# Alexa, Enjoy Your Retirement! — Illustration Style Guide

## Reusable image generation prompt

Create a landscape 3:2 header illustration for the blog series “Alexa, Enjoy Your Retirement!”

### Visual style

- Mid-century retro comic book meets vintage illustrated postcard.
- Bold, slightly irregular black ink outlines.
- Grainy Ben-Day dots, halftone shading, and subtle crosshatching.
- A limited, saturated color palette of turquoise, butter yellow, warm cream, orange-red, and deep black.
- Authentic vintage printing imperfections, subtle paper grain, and lightly distressed cream-colored edges.
- Warm, nostalgic atmosphere with a playful, slightly ironic editorial tone.
- Contemporary technology depicted as if illustrated in a 1950s magazine.

### Composition

- Large, immediately recognizable foreground objects.
- Simple background with a few carefully chosen environmental details.
- Strong visual hierarchy and clear silhouettes that remain readable at reduced blog-header size.
- Visual storytelling with understated humor, preferably through the contrast between modern technology and everyday human behavior.
- Recognizable hardware designs when depicting actual products.

### Constraints

- No photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, modern vector-art aesthetic, or smooth digital gradients.
- No titles, captions, speech bubbles, or decorative typography unless explicitly requested.
- The illustration should tell its story without words.

Use the previous illustrations in this blog series as style references only. Maintain consistent colors, line weight, print texture, and artistic treatment, but create an original composition for the new scene.

This gives the illustrations a distinctive identity while leaving room for technical subjects, everyday frustrations, and visual jokes.
